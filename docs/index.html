AI Agents

What is an AI Agent?

AI Agents are customizable AI assistants built to communicate with users and automate conversations. Create as many agents as you need and tailor their behavior, knowledge, and actions to fit your customer service goals.

Create a new AI Agent

Follow the steps below to create a new AI Agent:

  1. On the AI Agents page, click Create New (plus icon).
  2. In Create a New AI Agent, enter a Name, select a Template from the dropdown, and then click Create a New AI Agent. The AI Agent will appear on the dashboard after a few moments.
  3. On the AI Agent card, select Settings (gear icon) to configure your new AI Agent.

Want to learn more about configuring your AI Agent? Watch the demo video for a walkthrough of the available configuration options, including AI Behavior, Knowledge Base, and more.

AI Behavior

The first setting you can configure is AI Behavior. Use AI Behavior to define how your AI Agent communicates and responds to customers.

  • Welcome Message: Set the initial message the AI Agent sends at the start of a conversation.
  • AI Core Communication: Define the core communication guidelines for your AI Agent. For each setting, you can choose from preset options, enter your own instructions, or use Optimize to refine and improve your prompts.
    • Communication Style: Set how the AI Agent should communicate with customers.
      • Choose from presets such as Formal, Friendly, Empathetic, Concise, or Educational, or define your own communication style.
    • Conversation Flow: Define how the AI Agent should guide and manage conversations.
      • Choose from presets such as Problem Solving, Customer Service, Sales Agent, Finance, or Tutorial, or provide your own instructions.
    • Rules & Restriction: Set rules, limitations, and topics the AI Agent should follow or avoid.
      • Choose from presets such as Safe Tone, No Competitor, Compliance, Scope Limit, or Privacy Guard, or define your own rules.
  • Additional Instructions: Create targeted AI instructions for specific customer situations. Select a Pipeline to apply instructions only to customers in that pipeline, or leave the Pipeline empty to apply the instructions to all customers.
    • Pipeline: A Pipeline represents the customer's stage or status in the customer journey, helping you apply specific AI instructions based on where the customer is in the process. Select the customer pipeline to which the instructions should apply, such as New Leads, Hot Leads, Payment, or Customer.
    • Instructions: Add extra AI guidance for the selected pipeline or for all customer situations. Enter your own instructions or use Optimize to refine and improve your prompts.
NOTE: To add a new Pipeline:
  • Click Settings in the top-right navigation bar.
  • On the Settings page, click Pipeline Settings.
  • On the Pipeline Statuses page, click Add Status (plus icon).
  • On the Add Status pop-up, enter a Name and select a color or enter a hex color code. Click Add.
  • The new pipeline status will appear under Order Name and will be available in the pipeline dropdown when you return to Additional Instructions.

Versioning: After your conditions are saved, they will automatically be stored as the newest version of the AI Behavior setup. This ensures that any updates you make are preserved as the current active version. To review or switch between previous versions, use the Versions dropdown at the top of the configuration panel. This allows you to revisit earlier setups and compare changes.

NOTE: Only the newest saved version can be used. Older versions are available for reference only.

Knowledge Base

Next, use Knowledge Base to provide your AI Agent with business information to help answer customer questions accurately. You can add knowledge using the following formats:

  • Text: Add business or other relevant information directly as text for your AI Agent to learn from. You can organize your content by topic across different pages. Click Add Tab (plus icon) to create a new page and add additional information.
  • Website: Provide website content for your AI Agent to learn from. You can add a single webpage or collect multiple links from a website.
    • Batch Link: Use Web Link Collector to collect multiple pages from a website. Enter a starting URL and click Collect Link. The tool will gather up to 300 unique links from the site, excluding files. A Collected Links window will appear, where you can review or add links before training your AI Agent.
    • Single Link: Add an individual webpage for your AI Agent to learn from. Enter the URL in Add Single Link, then click Search Link to view its content directly. A Collected Links window will appear where you can review or add the link before training your AI Agent.
  • File: Upload PDF files containing business, product, or other relevant information for your AI Agent to learn from. You can drag and drop a file into the upload area or click to select a file from your device.
    • PDF Requirements: Make sure the text in your PDF can be highlighted or selected. Only text content is detected and processed. Images within the PDF are not supported.
  • Q&A: Add frequently asked questions and their answers to provide your AI Agent with clear, predefined responses.
    • Question: Enter the question a customer may ask.
    • Answer: Enter the answer the AI Agent should provide for that question.
    • Click Add New Q&A to create additional question-and-answer pairs.

AI Actions & Routing

Next, use AI Actions & Routing to automate how conversations are handled, categorized, and routed based on specific conditions.

  • Agent Transfer: Automatically transfer conversations to human agents when specific conditions are met. Click Add Condition (plus icon) to add additional conditions for agent transfers. You can choose from the following condition types:
    • Prompt: Enter instructions describing when the conversation should be transferred to a human agent. Use Optimize to have AI refine and improve the prompt.
    • Keywords: Enter keywords that trigger an agent transfer when they appear in the conversation.
    • Number of Messages: Set the number of messages in a session that must be reached before an agent transfer occurs.
  • Pending Status Messages: Set the messages customers receive while waiting for a human agent during the handoff process.
    • Pending - Assigned Message: Set the message shown to the customer while waiting for their assigned human agent.
    • Pending - Unassigned Message: Set the message shown to the customer while waiting to be assigned to a human agent.
  • Conversation Label: Automatically categorize conversations by applying labels when specific conditions are met. Click Add Condition (plus icon) to add additional conversational labels. You can use:
    • Prompt: Define the circumstances under which the label should be applied. Use Optimize to refine and improve the prompt.
    • Keywords: Enter keywords that trigger the label when they appear in the conversation.
    • Number of Messages: Apply the label after a specified number of messages in the session.
NOTE: You can select up to 10 labels for each condition.
NOTE: To add a new Label:
  • Click Settings in the top-right navigation bar.
  • On the Settings page, click Labels.
  • On the Labels page, click Add Labels (plus icon).
  • On the Add Status pop-up, enter a Name and select a Label Color, or enter a hex color code. Click Create.
  • The new label will appear in the Labels list and will be available in the Labels dropdown when you return to Conversation Label. You can edit the label name or color, or delete the label from the Settings page.
  • Conversation Pipeline: Automatically move conversations between pipeline stages when specific conditions are met. Click Add Condition (plus icon) to add additional conversation pipelines. You can use:
    • Current Pipeline: Select the pipeline stage where the conversation is currently located.
    • Target Pipeline: Select the pipeline stage where the conversation should move.
    • Prompt: Define the circumstances under which the conversation should move to another pipeline stage. Use Optimize to refine and improve the prompt.
    • Keywords: Enter keywords that trigger the pipeline change when they appear in the conversation.
    • Number of Messages: Move the conversation to another pipeline stage after a specified number of messages in the session.
    NOTE: To add a new Pipeline:
    • Click Settings in the top-right navigation bar.
    • On the Settings page, click Pipeline Settings.
    • On the Pipeline Statuses page, click Add Status (plus icon).
    • On the Add Status pop-up, enter a Name and select a color or enter a hex color code. Click Add.
    • The new pipeline status will appear under Order Name and will be available in the pipeline dropdown when you return to Additional Instructions.

    Versioning: After your conditions are saved, they will automatically be stored as the newest version of the AI Behavior setup. This ensures that any updates you make are preserved as the current active version. To review or switch between previous versions, use the Versions dropdown at the top of the configuration panel. This allows you to revisit earlier setups and compare changes.

    NOTE: Only the newest saved version can be used. Older versions are available for reference only.

    AI Configuration

    Use AI Configuration to configure the AI Agent's model, response behavior, context, and other settings that control how it processes and responds to conversations.

    General AI Settings
    • AI Model: Select the AI model used to generate responses. Different models may offer different capabilities and costs.
    • Stop AI After Transfer: Enable this setting to stop the AI Agent from sending responses after a conversation has been assigned to a human agent.
    • Silent Transfer: Enable this setting to transfer a conversation to a human agent without notifying the customer.
    • Timezone: Choose the timezone that the AI Agent will use for schedules and time‑based actions.
    Memory & Response Settings
    • AI History Limit: Set the number of previous messages the AI Agent can use as conversation context when generating a response.
    • AI Read File Limit: Set the number of recent attachments the AI Agent can read and reference during a conversation.
    • AI Context Limit: Set how much information from the Knowledge Base the AI Agent can use when generating responses.
    • AI Creativity Level: Control how creative or predictable the AI Agent's responses are. A more consistent setting produces more predictable responses, while higher creativity allows for more varied responses.
    • Message Await: Set how long the AI Agent waits before sending a response to a customer.
    • AI Message Limit: Set the maximum number of messages the AI Agent can send during a conversation.
    • Watcher: Monitor AI responses to help ensure important actions and rules are followed.

    Follow-ups

    Use Follow-ups to send automated messages to customers when they do not respond after a specified amount of time. The AI can use your prompt, chat history, and Knowledge Base to generate a relevant follow-up message.

    • Click Add Followup (plus icon) to create a new follow-up.
    • Prompt: Enter an instruction for the AI to use when generating the follow-up message. The prompt can also include conditions for handing the conversation off to a human agent.
    • Delay (min): Set the number of minutes to wait before sending the follow-up message.
    • Options: Expand Options to configure additional settings:
      • Send as exact message: Send the prompt as the exact message instead of having AI generate a follow-up based on the prompt.
      • Handoff to Agent After Sending: Automatically hand the conversation off to a human agent after the follow-up message is sent.
    • Image: Add an image to the follow-up message by clicking the image upload option.
    • Click Save Follow-up to save the follow-up.
    • To create another follow-up, click Add Follow-up (plus icon).

    Integrations

    Use Integrations to connect your AI Agent with supported platforms and services, extending its capabilities and connecting it to your existing customer service workflow.

    Orchestration

    Use Orchestration to enable AI Agents to hand off or switch between other AI Agents based on defined conditions. Configure parent and child agents to create intelligent routing workflows that automatically direct conversations to the most appropriate agent.

    Evaluation

    Finally, use Evaluation to view your AI Evaluation Documents and review how your AI Agent is performing against your evaluation criteria.
